Vehicle Sales Follow-Up Playbook: 4-Stage Cadence

A systematic multi-channel cadence across days 1, 3, 7, and 14 following a dealership showroom visit. Engineered to rekindle quiet leads without aggressive pressure.

By Marcus Vance, Sales Enablement Lead Updated October 2026
12 min read 3,420 views

Playbook Quick Navigation

  • 1. Phase 1: The First 2-Hour Window (The Impression Lock)
  • 2. Phase 2: Day 2–3 Personalized Video Touchpoint
  • 3. Phase 3: Day 7 Value Reconnect (New Trade-In or Trim Equity)
  • 4. Phase 4: Day 14 Soft Reset & Permission to Close
  • 5. Interactive Follow-Up Execution Checklist

Over 65% of qualified vehicle buyers leave the showroom without signing a contract on their initial visit. For decades, traditional sales training taught consultants to hound prospects daily with generic "Just checking in!" calls. In the modern automotive retail environment, this outdated cadence triggers instant lead resistance.

Core Principle

"Every follow-up touchpoint must deliver incremental utility. If your message contains no new vehicle detail, no financial clarity, and no helpful insight, you are asking for the customer's time without earning it."

Phase 1: The First 2-Hour Window (The Impression Lock)

Within 120 minutes of the customer driving off the dealership lot, the sensory memory of the test drive is peak. The purpose of Phase 1 is not to ask for a deal, but to validate their time and preserve emotional momentum.

Recommended SMS Script (Sent within 90 minutes)

"Hi [Customer Name], Marcus from DRIVEFORGE here. Thank you for test driving the [Vehicle Model] this afternoon. You mentioned wanting to verify how the golf bags fit in the rear cargo trunk — I measured it, and there is 38.2 cubic feet behind the second row. Let me know if you want me to snap a quick photo with standard luggage inside!"

Phase 2: Day 2–3 Personalized Video Touchpoint

If the prospect hasn't engaged by Day 2, do not send a boring plain-text email. Record a 30-second personalized smartphone walkaround focusing strictly on their top feature preference (such as the panoramic roof, lane centering assist, or wireless Apple CarPlay).

Video Walkaround Talk Track (30-45 Seconds)

"Hey [Customer Name]! Hope your week is off to a great start. I was walking the lot and saw the [Vehicle Model] in [Color] that we looked at. I remembered you had a question about how the head-up display adjusts for driver height, so I hopped inside to show you right on camera. Have a look! Zero rush at all — talk soon."

Phase 3: Day 7 Value Reconnect

By one week out, the shopper is usually cross-shopping competing dealerships or waiting for weekend availability. Bring new financial value to the table rather than repeating the original quote.

  • Trade Equity Re-check: "Our used inventory manager just requested two more [Trade-In Make/Year] units for our pre-owned lot. Would Saturday morning work to get you an updated appraisal bump?"
  • Factory Incentive Window: Highlight manufacturer APR subsidies or regional lease promotions that apply to their exact VIN.

Phase 4: Day 14 Soft Reset & Permission to Close

If no response has occurred after two weeks, give the prospect an honorable out. This psychological technique disarms tension and frequently provokes an honest response.

Permission-to-Close Email

"Hi [Customer Name], usually when I haven't heard back, it means one of three things: (1) You found another vehicle elsewhere, (2) Your timing shifted down the road, or (3) You are still interested but swamped with life right now. Which one are you? If you decided to hold off, no problem at all — I'll happily archive your file so I don't clutter your inbox."
