Customer Conversations Trending

How do you handle customers who only want to talk price before test driving?

Whenever a customer walks in and immediately says "Just give me your absolute lowest out-the-door price, I'm not doing a test drive today," what talk track do you use to regain value control without sounding evasive or combative?

#PriceStall #ShowroomGreeting #ValueFirst
640 views

Top Community Insight

Danielle Perez (General Sales Manager) 1 hour ago

"I tell them: 'I will gladly get you our very best price right on paper, Mr. Customer. My only job first is to make sure you actually love the vehicle, because even if I discounted it by 50%, the wrong car at any price is a waste of your hard-earned money. Let's do a 4-minute spin first.' That disarms them 85% of the time."

Best follow-up cadence after a test drive where the customer seemed enthusiastic but vanished?

We had a fantastic test drive on a new hybrid SUV. Customer smiled, complimented the ride, agreed on the color. Left saying they'd email their trade title that evening. 48 hours later, total silence. What video or text hook gets you the highest resurrection rate?

#GhostedLead #VideoText #TestDriveFollowUp
890 views

Top Community Insight

Corey Vance (Lead Closer) 3 hours ago

"Send a 12-second vertical video recorded from the driver's seat pointing at the instrument cluster: 'Hey John, was just pulling the hybrid back into our prep bay and wanted to double-check if your garage clearance was 78 inches like you mentioned. Give me a quick shout!' Questions about their space get answered 3x faster than 'just checking in'."

Negotiation

What's your approach to difficult trade-in conversations when online instant cash offers are unrealistically high?

Customers arriving with automated algorithmic instant offers from online national portals that didn't factor in curb rash, mismatched tires, and an illuminated check engine light. How do you bridge the expectation gap without insulting the customer's pride?

#TradeIn #Appraisal #UsedVehicles
1,140 views

Top Community Insight

Elena Rostova (Used Car Appraiser) 12 hours ago

"Do the silent walkaround with the customer holding the clipboard! When you find the tire tread at 2/32, let them touch it and say: 'If you were buying this car from me today, would you want brand new tires on it before taking your family home?' They nod, and they realize the reconditioning cost on their own."

How do you train green pea consultants to stop spewing feature checklists on test drives?
